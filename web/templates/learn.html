{{tr "Prefixado, IPCA+, and Tesouro Selic"}}

{{tr "Prefixado (LTN, without coupons)"}} {{tr "has a fixed nominal payment of BRL 1,000.00 per unit at maturity. Nominal means the amount is not adjusted for inflation; its purchasing power can change."}}

{{tr "IPCA+ (NTN-B Principal, without coupons)"}} {{tr "links its principal to inflation. Its quoted yield is a real annual rate, above inflation. A same-date scenario changes that real yield while holding the indexation anchor fixed."}}

{{tr "Does an IPCA+ scenario predict an inflation-linked return?"}}

{{tr "No. Future nominal amounts depend on future inflation. V1 does not assume an inflation path or project the nominal maturity value. Its same-date calculation does not reconstruct an official updated nominal value (VNA)."}}

{{tr "Tesouro Selic (LFT)"}} {{tr "follows the Selic rate. The yield field in its quote is a spread, not the current Selic rate. V1 displays official quotes, history, and portfolio valuation using official PU Base. Yield shocks, duration/convexity, and hold-versus-early-exit calculations are unsupported."}}

{{tr "Coupon-bearing titles, RendA+, Educa+, and other instrument types are unsupported in V1; their cash flows are not approximated with these formulas."}}

{{tr "Why do price and yield move in opposite directions?"}}

{{tr "With the payment assumptions and remaining term held fixed, a lower yield corresponds to a higher present price. A higher yield corresponds to a lower price. This relationship describes a calculation, not what yields will do next."}}

{{tr "Basis points (bps)"}} {{tr "describe yield changes: 100 bps = 1 percentage point. For example, changing an assumed yield from 10.88% to 8.88% is a shock of −200 bps, not a 2% relative reduction."}}

{{tr "Inspect the shared scenario formula"}}

scenario_PU = official_PU × ((1 + official_yield) / (1 + simulated_yield))^(DU / 252)

{{tr "PU means unit price. Yields in this formula are annual fractions; the Playground accepts percentages. DU is the verified financial-market business-day count, settlement inclusive and maturity exclusive, using the versioned ANBIMA calendar. Dates outside its coverage are unavailable."}}

{{tr "The calculation is anchored to the official PU/yield pair for the selected context. An unchanged yield preserves the official PU. Open Advanced in the Playground for exact inputs, source, dates, versions, and the CLI reproduction command. The chart and its numeric table use the same calculation."}}

{{tr "Maturity and the date of a value"}}

{{tr "Maturity is the bond's contractual payment date. An old pre-maturity quote is not a current value for a matured position. V1 shows that position as matured and does not reconcile actual maturity cash."}}

{{tr "The official quote date ("}}Data Base{{tr ") tells you when a quote applies. The import timestamp only tells you when it entered this database. An older quote is labeled; missing dates are never filled with a previous quote."}}

{{tr "Can I still explore a matured bond?"}}

{{tr "Yes, as an explicitly historical scenario. The selected quote and settlement dates determine the remaining term, even if the bond has matured since then. Scenarios require settlement before maturity and a positive verified business-day count."}}

{{tr "The portfolio's gross hold-versus-early-exit comparison is limited to Prefixado. Its break-even rate describes a hypothetical reinvestment condition, not an available offer. It is unsupported for IPCA+ and Selic."}}

{{tr "Gross values, net values, and predictions"}}

{{tr "Gross"}} {{tr "amounts exclude taxes and fees. A net amount would need the applicable costs and tax treatment; V1 has no complete tax engine and does not calculate net cash received."}}

{{tr "A scenario is a hypothesis."}} {{tr "“If the yield were X, holding the other scenario conditions constant…” changes an assumption. It does not forecast a market quote or recommend a financial action."}}

{{tr "Distinguish the official input, the hypothetical yield, and the derived result. The Playground keeps the quote date and settlement fixed. IPCA+ also keeps its same-date indexation anchor fixed; no future inflation is silently assumed."}}
