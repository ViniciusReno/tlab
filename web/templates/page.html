Why does the price respond?

A fixed-rate bond pays a fixed nominal amount at maturity. Holding the remaining term constant, a lower yield corresponds to a higher price; a higher yield corresponds to a lower price.

Here, only the assumed yield changes. Settlement remains {{.Result.SettlementDate}}, with {{.Result.BusinessDays}} business days to maturity.
